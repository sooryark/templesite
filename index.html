Arulmigu Alli Thurai Sri Kamakshi Amman Koodipattu Temple

The Arulmigu Alli Thurai Sri Kamakshi Amman Koodipattu Temple is one of the most revered shrines in Tamil Nadu. It is known for its divine grace and vibrant spiritual ambiance. This sacred temple has been a cornerstone of faith for devotees for generations, offering solace and blessings to all who visit.

Sri Vinayagar Subordinate Om Murugan Subordinate Trichy District, Allithurai, Atavathur Village Arulmigu Kamakshi Amman, Arulmigu Madurai Veeran, Arulmigu Karuppasamy Aku Kudipattu Temple Temple Ashtabandhana Maha Kumbabhishekam Ceremony Invitation Dear devotees, The auspicious Sopakrithu year will be celebrated on Monday, 11.09.2023, on the 25th day of the month of Avani, with the Dwadashi Tithi, Poosam Nakshatra and Siddha Yoga, at the Subayoga Subathinam, between 9.00 am and 10.30 am, in the Aries Lakkinam of Allithurai, Atavathur. Since the Maha Kumbabhishekam for the deities Sri Kamatchi Amman, Sri Madurai Veeran, and Sri Karuppasamy, the household deities of Karnam K. Ponnaiya Pillai and A. Veluppillai, will be held in a special way, we cordially invite all our partners, uncles, brothers-in-law, relatives, and village dignitaries to attend this festival and seek the blessings of Sri Kamatchi Amman of Allithurai and Atavathur. Sarvasagar: K.A. Saseetharan Panicker Venkateswara Swamigal Kerala.
